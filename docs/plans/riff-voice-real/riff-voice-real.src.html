Project: Riff (CreativeConvos) 2026-09-13 Revised after 2 adversarial reviews, awaiting Sean
The plan

The real app gets the lab's voice layer: Amoeba (you) and Burst (Riff), Ink & Wash, the fluid wash, and your paper with wash-tinted dots. It runs on your real mic and Riff's real audio, on top of the working Pen stream. The Pen keeps all sketch drawing; the lab's sparks, cinders, build particles and fake phone frames are cut.

The review changed the base. As drafted, the marks would have moved on the lab's fake sine wave even with a real mic, because Ink & Wash bypasses the audio hook. Stretching the lab's dot paper to full screen would also have stalled on zoom. Both are fixed below.

1. Tooltip (implementer + reviewer)

  • Remove native title from VoiceBar's icon buttons (VoiceBar.tsx:95) and Header (Header.tsx:45), same bug class. aria-label stays.
  • Add one shared tooltip anchored to the button, styled like the footnote popover already on main (white/95, zinc-200 border, shadow-lg).
  • Why not diagnose first: your browser isn't stock Chrome, and native tooltips are placed by the browser shell, so a Chrome test proves nothing. Removing the native tooltip fixes it in every browser.
  • Gate: hover capture shows the tooltip at the button; you confirm in your browser.

2. Voice layer on real audio (Opus builder + separate reviewer)

2a. Engine host mode, one named refactor; the lab must behave the same.

  • One level source levelFor(role) feeds every mark branch (human engine.ts:1478, Riff :1488, Shapeshift). It copies the SDK buffer before the talk blend, fires onsets whenever a source exists, and treats an empty buffer as "no source".
  • Transparent canvas cleared every frame; the engine paints no paper in host mode.
  • Job channel off: no frames, cinders, build, stream or scripted beats. The gate checks nothing job-driven still draws.
  • One copy of your tuned values in tuning.ts, read by both the lab panels and the real app. This fixes the 7 engine defaults that differ from your baked dials.
  • Calibration: per-role gain, floor and curve applied to the SDK data. The marks were tuned on synthetic levels; real audio is processed and resampled to 100-8000 Hz.
  • Loop sleeps when voice is idle and everything has settled; destroy() removes its hidden SVG and timers.

2b. Real-app mount.

  • Fixed 1440x900 stage (the lab's own coordinate model) anchored per call 1, scaled to the viewport width (min 0.5). It holds the marks canvas and the mask wrapper with the fluid wash, sits above the sketch per call 2, and ignores the pointer.
  • Anchor position cached, refreshed on resize and during the bar's chat-open slide, never read blind every frame.
  • State from the live session:
    • Riff speaking → Burst.
    • Listening → Amoeba while your input is above a threshold, silence otherwise.
    • Mic blocked → dead mic; everything else → idle.
    The yield squash fires on the server's end of turn (userTurnCount), not every pause. Riff's level drops to zero when he stops, since the SDK fades interrupted audio over 2s.
  • Dev tools: ?voiceState= fixtures set the engine state directly. __riffVoiceFeed pushes recorded audio frames through the level source. __riffVoicePerf samples frame timing. A dev-only ?dials=1 panel holds calibration and VAD values, baked after your pass.
  • Remove the bar's listening/speaking glow; keep the thinking glow for typed sketches (call 4).
  • Off-limits: render_artifact in useVoice.ts:119-153, sketch-job.ts, the Pen (draw-treatment.ts, canvas-slots.ts), API routes.

Gates (evidence).

  • Audio reaches the marks: the same frame with a quiet vs loud injected source shows a visibly different Burst under Ink & Wash. The recorded-audio feed moves the band readout.
  • Captures at 1440x900 and 390x844: idle, speaking over a mid-ink replay, listening, chat open, caption showing.
  • Stream untouched: __riffPen and __riffNodeRenders match feat/stream on the same replay; useVoice.ts handler diff is empty.
  • Performance in your real Chrome at your window size: replay, wheel zoom, the fit animation, 30s idle. Budget comes from the measured refresh rate.
  • Your live pass with a mic: calibration dials, then bake.

3. Paper (after you OK step 2)

  • Lattice: keep React Flow's own dot background, retuned to your paper values (pitch 12, dot 1, opacity 0.3). It stays crisp and pans and zooms for free.
  • Tinted dots: a small canvas inside React Flow, between the dots and the frames, redrawn in the same tick as the viewport. It only covers the wash's footprint and allocates nothing per dot.
  • Empty state: the same dots in CSS plus the same tint layer. main becomes relative isolate so nothing paints over the headline.
  • Remove the canvases' cyan/lime gradient.
  • Gate: captures at zoom 1, 0.6 and 0.15; a fit-animation recording shows tints locked to the dots; performance during wheel zoom.

4. Ship (on your nod)

  • Re-merge origin/main if it moved, rerun gates, push feat/voice-real:main with an explicit refspec.
  • Ships together: the 10 VoiceBar commits, the real stream, the voice layer, /voice-lab per call 3.
  • Verify: curl prod for a string only this build has; you run one live session on prod (streaming on Vercel prod is untested).
