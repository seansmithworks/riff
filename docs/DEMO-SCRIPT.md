# Riff — 3 Minute Demo Script

**Register key:** `TO THE ROOM` = spoken out to the judges. `TO RIFF` = spoken into the mic, conversational, like talking to a colleague. `[ACTION]` = what your hands do. Never mix the two registers in one breath. When you talk to Riff, turn slightly toward the screen. When you talk to the room, turn back and make eye contact. That physical turn is what sells the difference.

**Before you start:** system audio out through the room speakers so they hear Riff reply. Laptop, not phone. Browser already on the app, mic permission already granted, CopilotKit sidebar closed.

---

## Time budget

Stage pace is about 2.5 words per second. 180 seconds total.

| # | Beat | Clock | Words | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold open | 0:00–0:15 | 38 | slow, no slides |
| 2 | The turn | 0:15–0:26 | 20 | + 3s to click mic |
| 3 | First brief to Riff | 0:26–0:36 | 24 | spoken to Riff |
| 4 | Riff's question, your answer | 0:36–0:50 | 12 | Riff speaks ~7s |
| 5 | **Generation gap 1 (~14s)** | 0:50–1:04 | 40 | concept lands here |
| 6 | Artifact payoff | 1:04–1:14 | 26 | point at screen |
| 7 | The evolve ask | 1:14–1:27 | 22 | Riff acks ~4s |
| 8 | **Generation gap 2 (~19s)** | 1:27–1:46 | 55 | the real idea lands here |
| 9 | Money moment | 1:46–2:08 | 52 | pauses built in |
| 10 | Multimodal + honesty | 2:08–2:28 | 48 | |
| 11 | Close | 2:28–2:56 | 68 | |
| | **Total** | **2:56** | **~405** | 24s headroom |

Every second of generation wait is covered by a scripted line. There is no silence anywhere in this script.

---

## 1. Cold open — 0:00 to 0:15

No slide. Don't touch the laptop. Just talk.

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "Best idea I've had this year showed up while I was driving.
>
> No laptop. No Figma.
>
> So I talked it into my phone.
>
> What I got back was a transcript. A wall of text I never opened again."

*(Beat. One full second of silence after "again." Let it sit.)*

---

## 2. The turn — 0:15 to 0:26

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "Voice notes capture words. They don't capture design.
>
> So I built Riff. You talk. It draws.
>
> Let me show you."

`[ACTION] Click the mic. It goes coral. Turn toward the screen.`

---

## 3. First brief — 0:26 to 0:36

Slower, casual, like talking to a coworker standing next to you. Not performing.

> **TO RIFF**
>
> "Hey Riff. I want to design a booking app for dog walkers.
>
> Show me the flow a first time user goes through."

---

## 4. Riff's clarifying question — 0:36 to 0:50

Riff asks one question. You can't script what it asks, so here's a universal answer. Say it fast and move on.

> **TO RIFF**
>
> "Keep it simple. Mobile, first time user. Go ahead and draw it."

`[ACTION] Turn back to the room the moment you finish speaking. Do not watch the screen. If you watch the screen, the room watches the screen, and now everyone is waiting.`

---

## 5. Generation gap 1 — 0:50 to 1:04 (~14 seconds)

Facing the room, hands loose, screen behind you. The concept goes here. The wait does narrative work.

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "While that runs. This is an ElevenLabs voice agent, but it isn't taking dictation.
>
> It's got a design partner's job description. Ask one sharp question. Then draw.
>
> Fireworks turns what I just said into structured artifact JSON, and my own renderers draw it.
>
> Conversation is the interface. The artifact replaces the transcript."

---

## 6. Artifact payoff — 1:04 to 1:14

`[ACTION] Half-turn to the screen, gesture across the three phone frames, then back to the room.`

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "Three screens. From one sentence.
>
> That's fine. Any model can do that.
>
> Here's the part that isn't."

---

## 7. The evolve ask — 1:14 to 1:27

Back to conversational register. Say it plainly, like you just thought of it.

> **TO RIFF**
>
> "Add a screen where they pick a time slot and pay."

`[ACTION] Turn back to the room immediately. Riff acknowledges out loud, which buys a few seconds.`

---

## 8. Generation gap 2 — 1:27 to 1:46 (~19 seconds)

The longest wait and the most important thing you say. You're telling them what to look for before they see it.

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "Watch what it does not do.
>
> It's not starting over. Those three screens stay exactly as they are.
>
> It has to work out where a payment step belongs in this flow. And what that does to the screens around it.
>
> That's the difference between a generator and a collaborator.
>
> And yeah, this takes about twenty seconds. I'm not going to pretend it doesn't."

*(Say that last line dry, not apologetic. Naming the wait turns it into composure.)*

---

## 9. The money moment — 1:46 to 2:08

Slow down. This is the whole demo. Point at each thing physically. Three beats, pause after each.

`[ACTION] Turn to screen. Point at the original screens.`

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "Those three didn't change. Not regenerated. Identical.

`[ACTION] Point at the inserted screen.`

> The payment screen went in where it belongs. Not tacked on the end.

`[ACTION] Point at the relabeled button. Take a beat before you say it.`

> And this button. It used to say Confirm Booking.
>
> Now it says Continue to Payment.
>
> I never asked for that. It worked out that screen isn't the last step anymore."

*(Pause. Let them get it. Then:)*

> "Every one of these runs is logged to Braintrust, because the interesting question isn't whether it drew something. It's whether what it drew matches what I actually said."

---

## 10. Multimodal and honesty — 2:08 to 2:28

`[ACTION] Open the CopilotKit sidebar. Don't type, just show it.`

> **TO THE ROOM**
>
> "Voice isn't always the right input. Open office, loud room, bad day.
>
> So the same loop runs typed, through CopilotKit. Same agent, same artifact.
>
> And to be straight with you: these are wireframes. Low fidelity. This doesn't replace design work.
>
> It replaces the twenty minutes you lose getting the idea out of your head."

---

## 11. Close

### Ending A — Daytona shipped

**Staging:** click "Hand off to build" at **2:15**, during the multimodal block, so the sandbox spins up while you talk. Do not click at 2:28 and stand there waiting.

> **TO THE ROOM** *(2:28 to 2:56)*
>
> "One more thing. I fired this off thirty seconds ago.

`[ACTION] Bring the handoff result forward. Click through to the live URL.`

> That's a Daytona sandbox. It took the artifact and handed me back something running.
>
> So the whole arc is: idea shows up in the car. Artifact on screen. Something you can click.
>
> I never opened a canvas.
>
> Riff. Design out loud."

**If the sandbox hasn't returned by 2:45:** don't wait. Say "that's still cooking" and go straight into Ending B's last four lines.

### Ending B — No Daytona

> **TO THE ROOM** *(2:28 to 2:56)*
>
> "Where this goes next is the part I keep thinking about.
>
> The artifact is structured data, not a picture. So it can hand off. To a sandbox, to a repo, to running code.
>
> Which means the idea you had in the car is something you can click by the time you park.
>
> That's the whole thing. Talk it, see it, ship it.
>
> Riff. Design out loud."

## Failure mode card

One rule above all: **acknowledge once, then move.** Never apologize twice. Never say "it usually works." Never troubleshoot facing the laptop. Whatever breaks, your body stays turned to the room.

### Voice won't connect / mic dead

> "Wifi at a hackathon. Let's do it the other way."

`[ACTION] Open the CopilotKit sidebar. Type:` **"Booking app for dog walkers. Mobile, first time user flow."** `Enter.`

> "Same agent, same artifact, typed instead of spoken. This is what I meant about multimodal."

The demo continues from beat 5 unchanged. Only beats 3 and 7 move from voice to typing.

### Generation errors out

First failure, one line, no apology:

> "That one dropped. Run it again."

Second failure, go to the sample (dev preview buttons, top right):

> "Fine. Here's one I made this morning, exact same way."

`[ACTION] Click "Wireframe" in the header.` Carry straight into beat 6. The gap lines still work on a pre-made artifact — they're about how it thinks, not about it thinking live.

### Taking way too long (past ~25 seconds)

Banked lines, one at a time, ~6 seconds each. Stay facing the room.

1. "This is a fast model doing structured output, and structure is the slow part. Free text streams. Layout doesn't."
2. "I could fake this with a loading animation and pre-baked screens. I'd rather show you the real number."
3. "For what it's worth, this is still faster than opening Figma."

Past 35 seconds, cut losses out loud:

> "Not today. Here's the one from this morning."

### Riff talks over you or won't stop

`[ACTION] Click the mic to end the session.`

> "It's a design partner. It's got opinions."

Then finish in the sidebar. That line gets a laugh and buys the room back.

### Running over time

Cut in this order, no hesitation:

1. The Braintrust line at the end of beat 9.
2. The whole multimodal block, beat 10.
3. Ending A's setup — jump straight to "Riff. Design out loud."

**Never cut beat 8 or beat 9.** The gap-2 explanation and the relabeled button are the demo. Everything else is context.

---

## Rehearsal checks

1. **Time your actual gaps.** If gap 1 lands nearer 11 seconds than 14, cut the "Fireworks turns what I just said" line so you're not stranded mid-sentence when the artifact pops.
2. **The button relabel must be readable from the back row.** If a judge can't read "Continue to Payment" from their seat, zoom the browser to 125% before you start. That one word change is the entire argument.
